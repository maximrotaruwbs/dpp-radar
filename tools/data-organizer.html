FAQ

Is the DPP Data Organizer free?
Yes, free to use with no sign-up. You can tag fields and export a CSV or print a copy without providing an email address.
Where are my tags saved?
Only in your own browser's local storage. We don't see or store your tags on a server - they'll still be there if you come back on the same device and browser, but clearing your browser data will remove them.
What export formats are supported?
A CSV download (category, field, status) generated entirely in your browser, and a print-friendly view you can save as a PDF using your browser's own print dialog.
