DPP Tools & Software Directory

A filterable directory of the organisations, standards and open-source projects operating in the Digital Product Passport space today: official EU infrastructure, DPP issuer platforms, digital wallet providers, data-carrier standards, and open-source frameworks.

Listed for reference only - inclusion isn't an endorsement, we don't accept paid placement, and this isn't a ranked or exhaustive list. Vendor landscapes change quickly; always verify current details directly with each provider. Directory last checked 31 Jul 2026.

EU Digital Product Passport Hub (European Commission) Issuer / platform

The European Commission's own policy portal for the Digital Product Passport under the Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation (ESPR) - the primary official reference for scope, timelines and requirements.

Visit official page ↗
EU Digital Identity Wallet (EUDI Wallet) Wallet

The European Commission's official page on the EU Digital Identity Wallet - the eIDAS 2.0 credential wallet every member state must offer by December 2026, and a likely building block for verifying DPP-linked credentials.

Visit official page ↗
GS1 Digital Link Data carrier

GS1's global standard for encoding product identifiers into QR codes and barcodes, so a single scan can resolve to a product's Digital Product Passport - widely referenced as a candidate DPP data-carrier format.

Visit official page ↗
CIRPASS-2 Open-source

An EU-funded project piloting Digital Product Passports across textiles, electronics, tyres and construction, and building an open-source test environment for DPP data interoperability.

Visit project site ↗
Eclipse Tractus-X - Digital Product Pass Open-source

An open-source reference implementation for visualising Digital Product Passports, Battery Passports and Transmission Passports, developed as part of the Eclipse Tractus-X automotive dataspace project.

Visit project site ↗
walt.id WalletOpen-source

An open-source identity and digital-wallet toolkit supporting eIDAS 2.0 / EUDI Wallet credential issuance and verification use cases.

Visit official site ↗
Spherity Issuer / platform

A commercial software platform for issuing, managing and verifying Digital Product Passports and related supply-chain credentials.

Visit official site ↗
Circularise Issuer / platform

A commercial traceability and Digital Product Passport software platform used by manufacturers to share verified product data across their supply chain.

Visit official site ↗
osapiens Issuer / platform

A commercial sustainability and supply-chain software platform with Digital Product Passport data-management features.

Visit official site ↗
Avery Dennison atma.io Issuer / platformData carrier

A connected-product platform combining physical data carriers (RFID, QR) with software for building and managing Digital Product Passports.

Visit official site ↗
