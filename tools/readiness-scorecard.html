DPP Readiness Scorecard

A short, structured assessment of how ready your organisation is for the EU Digital Product Passport, across data, suppliers, systems and process. Answer 12 questions and get an instant 0-100 score with a breakdown by category - right here, nothing to install or sign up for.

FAQ

Is the DPP Readiness Scorecard really free?
Yes. It's free to use, with no sign-up and no email required to see your score.
Do you store or see my answers?
No. The quiz runs entirely in your browser - your answers and score are never sent to a server unless you choose to use the optional "send my results to the team" form at the end.
How is the score calculated?
Twelve questions across four categories - data & documentation, suppliers & supply chain, systems & technology, and process & ownership - each scored 0-3 points. The total is scaled to a 0-100 readiness score, with a breakdown per category and a ranked action list.

This tool runs entirely in your browser - your answers aren't sent anywhere or stored on our servers. Refreshing the page resets the quiz.
